A leaner HUD, and appearance presets replaced with independent toggles.

## Changed

- **The HUD no longer shows the voice channel name** — it's just the member rows now (the panel still names the channel when you summon it). Your own mute/deafen state shows on your member row.
- **The v0.5.0 "HUD style" preset is now separate toggles**, so every combination works:
  - **HUD avatars** — on/off (off = names only, tighter rows, speakers highlighted in mint)
  - **HUD shows** — everyone / only speakers (with only-speakers, the HUD disappears completely while nobody talks)
  - **HUD opacity** — unchanged, 50–95 %, still HUD-only
  
  An existing `hudMode` in your config is migrated to the equivalent toggles automatically.
