      CouchCord first-run setup

      CouchCord talks to your running Discord app through a personal Discord application — free, takes about two minutes, and only works for your own account.

      1. and sign in with the same account your Discord app uses.
      2. Click New Application and name it anything (e.g. "CouchCord").
      3. On the OAuth2 page, under Redirects click Add Redirect, enter exactly http://127.0.0.1 and Save Changes.
      4. Still on OAuth2: copy the Client ID and Client Secret (click Reset Secret if it's hidden) and paste them below.

      Stored locally in — the secret is only ever sent to Discord itself. On the next start, Discord will show an authorization popup: click Authorize.
